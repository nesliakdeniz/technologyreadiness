Privacy Policy

Farm Tech Ready. Last updated August 1, 2026.

Farm Tech Ready does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. Farm Tech Ready does not collect any personal information, usage data, or device identifiers. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no registration.

Your assessment answers

The ratings and notes you enter stay on your own device while you use the app. They are never transmitted to us or to anyone else. When you close the app or start over, they are cleared. If you want to keep a record of your results, you can print or save them yourself from the results screen.

Analytics and tracking

Farm Tech Ready contains no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. We do not use cookies or similar technologies in the app.

Links to other websites

The app links to research-based resources published by the University of Wisconsin-Madison Division of Extension and, in a few cases, to other organizations such as academic journals and video platforms. When you tap a link, it opens in your browser and you leave the app. Those websites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or practices.

Children

Farm Tech Ready is intended for farmers, farm advisors, educators, and others making agricultural technology decisions. Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date shown above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about Farm Tech Ready can be sent to nesli@wisc.edu.
